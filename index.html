How it works

Three parts, one idea: nobody defends alone.

01

The registry

Every known attack on AI agents, with a stable ID, a mapping to MITRE ATLAS and OWASP, a harmless test that proves it, and a detection that stops it.

02

The sensor

A checkpoint between your agents and the tools they use. It enforces the registry's detections, works offline, and never takes your agent down if it fails.

03

In design

The network

When one member's sensor catches something new, a verified, anonymized signature protects every other member within hours, and goes to the lab or maintainer who can fix it.
